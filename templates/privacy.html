Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

StudyFlow is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is processed when using the StudyFlow application.

2. Data Storage & Management

All data created within StudyFlow—including tasks, study schedules, notes, focus session logs, and quiz results—is stored locally in a SQLite database (studyflow.db).

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

StudyFlow operates locally on your system. We do not sell, share, transmit, or license your personal information or study data to third-party services or data brokers.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You retain full control over your data. Individual tasks, study sessions, and notes can be deleted directly within the interface. To perform a complete data reset, you may delete the local database file (studyflow.db) while the application is stopped.

5. Security Limitations

While StudyFlow utilizes standard relational database queries and parameterized statements to protect against common database vulnerabilities, local database files rely on your operating system's filesystem permissions for physical access security.

6. Developer Contact Method

If you have questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding StudyFlow, please contact the development team at support@studyflow.local.
