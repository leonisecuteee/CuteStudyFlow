About StudyFlow

Empowering students with an integrated productivity hub.

Application Purpose

StudyFlow is designed to streamline academic workflows by combining essential study tools into a single, cohesive dashboard. From tracking task deadlines and organizing daily study sessions to writing notes, practicing subject quizzes, and tracking overall productivity statistics, StudyFlow brings organization to every aspect of learning.

Target Audience

StudyFlow is tailored for high school, undergraduate, and graduate students, as well as self-directed learners who seek an efficient, clutter-free environment to manage coursework, monitor academic goals, and optimize daily study habits.

Key Modules

  • Task Manager: Organize coursework with subject tags, priorities, due dates, and real-time status filtering.
  • Study Planner: Map out daily and weekly study sessions with precise start/end times.
  • Notes & Reviewers: Maintain structured course summaries and study notes with subject categorization.
  • Practice Quiz: Test retention with categorized multiple-choice quizzes and detailed explanations.
  • Progress Tracker: Monitor real-time completion rates, focus hours, overdue items, and quiz statistics.
